Hand gestures
  • 🤏
    Pinch and moveThumb and index together, then move your hand to rotate the globe. Let go to release.
  • 🤏🤏
    Pinch with both handsPull your hands apart to zoom in, bring them together to zoom out.
  • ☝️
    Point and holdHover the circle over a storm or alert for 1 second to open its details.
  • ✊
    FistStops any spin and closes the details card.

Tips: keep the other fingers open when you pinch, use even front lighting, and sit about an arm's length from the camera. Mouse also works: drag to rotate, Shift-drag or right-drag to move, scroll to zoom, click.

Data: GDACS (UN/EC), NASA GIBS (GOES, Himawari, NOAA-20, GPM IMERG, Blue Marble), Natural Earth, Open-Meteo, Wikipedia. Not an official warning source. Based on Earth in Your Hand by n07eo.
