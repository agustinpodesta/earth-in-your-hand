The inspiration

Why I built this

I've always been fascinated by watching natural disasters happen in real life using apps like Zoom Earth.

I thought, why not make the experience a little more fun? Computer vision has been going on for a while now, but it always felt like something only developers got to experiment with.

These days all you need is an AI agent, a webcam, and a browser to track your hands, so pretty much anyone who's into our planet can build something with it.

You can grab the Earth, spin it, zoom in with both hands, and point at a storm to see what's going on down there: how many people it's expected to affect, its forecasted location, and the local news around it.

Hope you have as much fun using it as I had making it.
